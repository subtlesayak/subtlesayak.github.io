# Region-Based E-Ink Refresh Summary

Replaced the page-wide grayscale overlay with staggered black/white waveform phases on visible content regions.

## Research Applied

- Global updates affect every pixel and flash; fast/partial updates affect changed pixels or a defined update window.
- E-paper particles require multiple timed frames/stages to reach and hold their target state.
- Fast updates compare previous and new image buffers; compensation or inverted updates are used to reduce ghosting.

Primary references:

- https://docs.pervasivedisplays.com/knowledge/Technology/updating-the-display.html
- https://docs.pervasivedisplays.com/knowledge/Hardware/troubleshooting/ghosting/fast-update-data-comparison.html
- https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4e/2.13inch_e-Paper_V4_Specification.pdf
