# Uniform About Production Cards

## Goal

Make Experience, Education, Projects, and Certificates cards share one predictable alignment, image frame, content start, and responsive rhythm without changing their content.

## Tasks

1. Give production images a stable media frame and semantic text classes in the renderer.
2. Standardize card grid dimensions, spacing, typography, and mobile behavior in CSS.
3. Bump cache versions and verify syntax, content, routes, and light/dark styling contracts.
